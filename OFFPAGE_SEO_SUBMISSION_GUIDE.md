## Off-Page SEO Safety Rules

- Do not buy bulk backlinks.
- Do not use automated backlink generators.
- Do not post the same keyword-stuffed text on hundreds of sites.
- Do not create fake reviews.
- Prioritise verified local citations and real customer reviews.
- Keep NAP details identical everywhere.
- Add photos and service details where possible.
- Build links slowly and naturally over several weeks.
